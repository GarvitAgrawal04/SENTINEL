## Becoming a Maintainer

Maintainer nominations follow the process in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md):

1. Sustained, high-quality contributions over at least 3 months.
2. Nomination by an existing maintainer.
3. Approval by existing maintainers (lazy consensus, 72-hour window).
4. Access provisioned to relevant registries and CI environments.

All maintainers agree to follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
